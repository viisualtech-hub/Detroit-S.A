Warning signs

Signs your vehicle needs attention

Gearbox

  • Slipping or jumping out of gear
  • Delayed or harsh shifting
  • Grinding or whining noises
  • Fluid leaks

Engine

  • Overheating
  • Loss of power
  • Knocking or unusual noises
  • Smoke or oil leaks

Diff

  • Whining or howling noise
  • Clunks when pulling away or turning
  • Vibration
  • Oil leaking at the diff

Not sure what's wrong? Call us on 031 577 0160 and describe the symptoms.
